Measuring how minds and systems perform under real-world conditions.

I design and analyze experiments, and develop evaluation frameworks for behavior in noisy, high-variance settings. My work uses dogs and humans as comparative models to ask how learners perceive input, form categories, generalize across contexts, and recover signal from degraded input. Those findings then translate to more applied detection and decision systems.

I am especially interested in evaluation: how to test whether a system, biological or otherwise, will hold up when conditions shift away from the ones it was trained on.
