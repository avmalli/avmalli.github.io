Measuring how minds and systems perform under real-world conditions.

I design experiments, build statistical models, and develop evaluation frameworks for behavior in noisy, high-variance settings. My work uses dogs and humans as comparative models to ask how learners form categories, generalize across contexts, and recover signal from degraded input — and translates those findings into applied detection and decision systems.

I am especially interested in evaluation: how to test whether a system — biological or otherwise — will hold up when conditions shift away from the ones it was trained on.
