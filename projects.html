Projects

Empirical research on how people and learned systems acquire, generalize, and apply knowledge under real-world conditions — with a focus on experimental design, evaluation frameworks, and measurement validation.

Measuring Cognitive Outcomes Under Real-World Conditions

Decade of empirical work designing studies that measure how learning, attention, and recognition hold up when conditions diverge from training. Spans infants, monolingual and second-language adults, clinical populations (ADHD), and dogs as a comparative model. Methods include behavioral paradigms (headturn preference, flanker, task-switching, working memory), EEG/ERP, and longitudinal field studies.

  • Experimental design
  • Cognitive outcomes
  • Behavioral measurement

Evaluation Frameworks for Learned Systems

Multi-year program designing evaluation systems for trained classifiers — in this case, biological detectors — including training-aid validation, candidate selection, generalization testing, and operational performance metrics. Five published or in-press papers and three federally funded grants. The framework generalizes: how do you tell whether a learned system will hold up when the test distribution diverges from training?

  • Evaluation
  • Generalization
  • Validation

Inter-Rater Reliability & Measurement Validation

Studies on observer agreement and rating reliability for behavioral and physiological measurements, including body condition scoring across 43 working explosives detection dogs (Christie et al., 2024) and perceptual rating accuracy for heat-related illness in dogs. The same methodological core that underlies rubric calibration, model-as-judge evaluation, and grader validation.

  • Measurement
  • Inter-rater reliability
  • Rubric design

Predictive Modeling of Behavioral Outcomes

Built and evaluated supervised machine learning models (XGBoost, gradient boosting) linking behavioral and physiological measurements to downstream outcomes, including a mentored project predicting working dog career trajectories from behavioral surveys. Focus on model fit, generalization, and stability under noisy real-world data.

  • Machine learning
  • Model evaluation
  • XGBoost

Perception Under Adverse Conditions

Comparative studies of speech recognition under noise and signal degradation in humans and dogs. One key finding: dogs recognize their name in multitalker noise at signal-to-noise ratios where 12-month-old infants fail; this result helps disentangle contributing auditory and attentional factors that impact infant speech perception in noise. This research connects to broader questions the impact of different types of noise and degradation on perception and categorization.

  • Speech perception
  • Signal degradation
  • Comparative cognition

Olfactometer Software & Data Pipeline

Redesigned research-grade control software for portable automated olfactometers. Rewrote validation logic and backend safeguards, added defensive input handling and mid-session recovery, and built automated Airtable upload workflows that standardize outputs for downstream statistical analysis.

  • Python
  • Pipeline engineering
  • Hardware integration
