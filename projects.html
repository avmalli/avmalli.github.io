Projects

Empirical research on how learners acquire and generalize information under real-world conditions, with a focus on meaningful experimental design, evaluation frameworks, and measurement validation.

Evaluating Frontier Vision-Language Models Against a Human Expertise Gradient

Four frontier vision-language models (Claude Opus 4.7, Claude Sonnet 4.6, GPT-5.5, GPT-4.1) evaluated on a visual ordinal clinical task (evaluation of body condition score) measured against a human expertise gradient generated from 1,010 survey participants. A GLMM with Holm-corrected contrasts was used to compare model performance against humans.

  • Model evaluation
  • VLMs
  • Calibration

Measuring Cognitive Outcomes Under Real-World Conditions

Decade of empirical work designing studies that measure the impact of real-world noise on perception and category learning. This research spans infants, monolingual and bilingual adults, clinical populations (ADHD), and dogs as a comparative model. Methods include behavioral paradigms (headturn preference, preferential looking), cognitive assessments (cognitive control and working memory tests), EEG/ERP, and longitudinal field studies.

  • Experimental design
  • Cognitive outcomes
  • Behavioral measurement

Evaluation Frameworks for Learned Systems

Multi-year program designing evaluation systems for trained classifiers (in this case, both trained dogs as biological detectors as well as algorithms parsing gas chromatography data) including training-aid validation, candidate selection, generalization testing, and operational performance metrics. Five published or in-press papers stemming from three federally funded grants and a state-funded grant. The framework examines the accuracy of trained detectors when the test distribution diverges from the training set.

  • Evaluation
  • Generalization
  • Validation

Inter-Rater Reliability & Measurement Validation

Studies on observer agreement and rating reliability for difficult-to-measure behavioral and physiological states, including body condition scoring across 43 working explosives detection dogs (Christie et al., 2024) and perceptual rating accuracy for heat-related illness in dogs. The same methodological core that underlies rubric calibration, model-as-judge evaluation, and grader validation.

  • Measurement
  • Inter-rater reliability
  • Rubric design

Perception Under Adverse Conditions

Comparative studies of speech recognition under noise and signal degradation in humans and dogs. One key finding: dogs recognize their name in multitalker noise at signal-to-noise ratios where 12-month-old infants fail; this result helps disentangle contributing auditory and attentional factors that impact infant speech perception in noise. This research connects to broader questions relating to the impact of different types of noise and degradation on perception and categorization.

  • Speech perception
  • Signal degradation
  • Comparative cognition

Olfactometer Software & Data Pipeline

Redesigned research-grade software in Python for portable automated olfactometers (electronic scent detection boxes). Rewrote validation logic and backend safeguards, added defensive input handling and mid-session recovery, and built automated Airtable upload workflows that standardize outputs for statistical analysis.

  • Python
  • Pipeline engineering
  • Hardware integration
